## The 30-Second Script Formula

Every short-form video follows the same architecture:

- **0–3 sec, Hook.** Text on screen + spoken. Stop the scroll or die. ("This $450K house has a $700K kitchen.")
- **3–25 sec, Value.** The tour, the tip, the story. One idea per video.
- **25–30 sec, CTA.** One clear action. ("Follow for daily [city] tours." "DM 'GUIDE' for the checklist.")

AI writes 30 scripts in one session from this formula. The prompt:

> "Write 10 thirty-second video scripts for [topic/listing]. Each: hook (under 10 words, text-on-screen + spoken), 3-4 beat outline for the middle, CTA (under 8 words). Conversational, energetic, no jargon."

## 7 Video Formats (and Their Scripts)

**1. Listing tours.** The 60-second walkthrough: hook + 3 highlights + CTA. Film on your phone, gimbal optional.

**2. Myth-busting.** "3 lies Zillow told you." High share rate, authority-building.

**3. Market updates.** Monthly numbers in 60 seconds. Your signature series.

**4. Neighborhood tours.** Driving commentary, relocation buyers' favorite content.

**5. Day-in-the-life.** Relatable authority. "8am: showing. 11am: negotiation. 3pm: closing gifts."

**6. Educational.** "What is earnest money?", 30-second explainers that get saved.

**7. POV/story.** "POV: your buyer just got the keys." Emotional, shareable.

AI scripts all seven formats. ([Instagram system](/blog/art-15-instagram-content-realtors-ai.html))

## Hooks: The First 3 Seconds

AI generates hooks in batches of 20. The formulas that work:

- **The number:** "This $389K home sold for $440K. Here's why."
- **The secret:** "The listing photos are hiding something."
- **The direct call:** "Stop scrolling if you're renting in [city]."
- **The contrarian:** "Don't buy a house in 2026. (Here's the exception.)"
- **The question:** "What does $500K buy in [city] right now?"

Generate 20, use the best 5, repeat weekly.

## The Batch Filming System

**Monthly (2 hours):** AI generates 30 scripts. You film 8–10 in one location block (same outfit = same day illusion, or change shirts between batches).

**Weekly (30 min):** edit on your phone (CapCut templates), add captions, schedule.

**Daily (5 min):** post + engage with comments.

Ten videos a month from 3 hours of work. That's the math that makes video sustainable.

## The Talking Head (Simplest Format, Highest Trust)

No tour, no B-roll, just you, talking to camera, sharing one idea. The simplest video format and often the highest-converting:

**The setup:** phone at eye level, window light on your face, 60 seconds.

**The structure:** hook (5 sec) → one idea (45 sec) → CTA (10 sec).

**The topics:** market takes, myth-busting, tips, stories, opinions. AI generates 30 talking-head topics monthly: "Give me 30 talking-head video topics for a [city] realtor: mix of market, education, opinion, and personal."

Talking heads build parasocial trust, followers feel they know you. When they're ready to transact, the known agent wins.
