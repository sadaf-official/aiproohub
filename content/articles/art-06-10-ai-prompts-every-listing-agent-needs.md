## 1. The Hero Listing Prompt

The workhorse. Full MLS description from your fact sheet — role, facts, format, voice, guardrails in one prompt. Use on every listing. (Full process: [MLS descriptions step by step](/blog/art-05-mls-descriptions-ai-step-by-step.html).)

## 2. The Opener Generator

Ten scroll-stopping first sentences per listing. The opener decides whether buyers keep reading — never settle for the first draft when you can choose from ten.

## 3. The Luxury Adapter

Takes your standard description and elevates it: refined vocabulary, lifestyle framing, prestige cues. Essential above $750K. ([Luxury copy guide](/blog/art-16-ai-luxury-listing-copy.html))

## 4. The Compliance Screener

Reviews any copy for fair-housing risk, flags problematic phrases, suggests safe rewrites. Run on every listing before publishing. ([Full compliance guide](/blog/art-02-fair-housing-safe-ai-listing-copy.html))

## 5. The Voice-Match Prompt

Paste your best past writing; AI studies your rhythm and tone. Every future output sounds like you. Three minutes, permanent fix for robotic AI copy. ([Voice training method](/blog/art-11-make-ai-sound-like-you-voice-training.html))

## 6. The Social Calendar Prompt

Thirty days of post ideas from one listing: captions, Reel hooks, carousel topics, story ideas. The consistency engine. ([30-day plan](/blog/art-04-30-day-ai-content-plan-agents.html))

## 7. The Just-Listed Caption Writer

Photo + prompt = Instagram-ready caption with hook, highlights, CTA, and hashtags. The posts that get saves are the posts that get showings.

## 8. The Seller Update Email

Weekly seller emails that prevent anxious 10pm texts: showings, feedback themes, next steps. Confidence in an inbox. ([Email sequences](/blog/art-14-real-estate-email-sequences-ai.html))

## 9. The Market Stat Transformer

Turns any MLS stat into a readable post with buyer takeaway, seller takeaway, and your expert read. Authority in one prompt. ([Newsletter guide](/blog/art-09-market-newsletters-ai-get-opened.html))

## 10. The Ad Copy Prompt

Twenty ad variations from one listing — headlines, primary text, CTAs — for Facebook and Google. Pre-qualifying copy that attracts showings, not just clicks. ([Facebook ads guide](/blog/art-10-real-estate-facebook-ads-ai.html))

## Example: The Hero Listing Prompt Framework

Here's the skeleton (fill the brackets with your listing facts):

> You are an experienced real estate listing copywriter known for warm, story-driven descriptions. Write a 180-word MLS description for [ADDRESS], a [BEDS]-bed/[BATHS]-bath [STYLE] built in [YEAR]. Key features: [FEATURE 1], [FEATURE 2], [FEATURE 3]. Neighborhood: [2-3 anchors]. Tone: [warm and inviting / luxury-polished / conversational]. Open with a sensory detail, not "Welcome to." Describe the property, never the people — fair-housing compliant. End with a soft call to action for a private showing. Do not invent features not listed here.

That single framework, customized per property, replaces an hour of blank-page staring. The remaining nine prompts follow the same anatomy: role, facts, format, voice, guardrails. (The full [prompt anatomy breakdown](/blog/ai-prompts-for-real-estate-agents.html) shows why each element matters.)

## Example: The Opener Generator Framework

> Write 10 distinct opening sentences for a listing description of [ADDRESS + 1 standout feature]. Vary the approach: 3 sensory, 3 story-driven, 2 bold/contrarian, 2 curiosity-driven. Each under 20 words. No "Welcome to" or "Nestled in."

Pick your favorite, delete the rest. Your listing now opens stronger than 95% of the MLS.

## Example: The Compliance Screener Framework

> Review this listing description for fair-housing compliance: [PASTE]. Flag any phrase referencing or implying race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, national origin, or age. For each flag, explain the risk in one sentence and suggest a compliant rewrite describing the property instead.

Sixty seconds. Every listing. Non-negotiable. (The [complete risky-phrase list](/blog/art-02-fair-housing-safe-ai-listing-copy.html) has 25 examples to study.)
