The 30-Day AI Content Plan for Real Estate Agents

Meta: A day-by-day 30-day content calendar for agents using AI — what to post, what prompts to use, and the batch-day system that produces it all in 4 hours.

"What should I post today?" is the question that kills more agent marketing than any algorithm change. This plan answers it for the next 30 days — with the exact AI prompts to generate each piece and the batch-day system that produces the whole month in one 4-hour session.

The Weekly Rhythm (Memorize This)

  • Monday: Market authority (stat, update, or explainer)
  • Tuesday: Listing showcase (tour, feature, or story)
  • Wednesday: Education (buyer/seller tip)
  • Thursday: Behind-the-scenes (you, humanized)
  • Friday: Community (local love, business spotlight)
  • Saturday: Story (client narrative, permission granted)
  • Sunday: Rest or reshare (repurpose the week's best performer)

Seven content pillars, rotating forever. You'll never wonder "what do I post" again.

Days 1–7: Foundation Week

Day 1 (Mon): Market stat post. Prompt: "Turn this MLS stat [STAT] into an Instagram post with buyer takeaway, seller takeaway, and my expert read. Conversational tone." (Market stat prompt guide)

Day 2 (Tue): Listing tour Reel. Script prompt from your feature list. (Video scripts)

Day 3 (Wed): "3 things buyers get wrong about pre-approval." Educational carousel prompt.

Day 4 (Thu): Day-in-the-life story. Prompt structures your raw notes into narrative.

Day 5 (Fri): Local business spotlight. (Partnership content ideas)

Day 6 (Sat): Client story (anonymized). Story-structure prompt.

Day 7 (Sun): Reshare the week's top performer with a new hook.

Days 8–14: Momentum Week

Day 8: "Buyer's vs. seller's market?" explainer from your inventory numbers.

Day 9: Before/after listing copy (generic vs. engineered — show the difference).

Day 10: "What is earnest money?" plain-English explainer.

Day 11: Open house announcement sequence.

Day 12: Neighborhood hidden gem.

Day 13: "My biggest mistake as a new agent" (vulnerability post).

Day 14: Reshare + weekly market thought.

Days 15–21: Authority Week

Day 15: Monthly market update video (script template).

Day 16: CMA story — "how we priced this one."

Day 17: "5 questions to ask at an open house."

Day 18: Stager/vendor spotlight.

Day 19: "Cost of waiting" analysis (rent vs. buy math).

Day 20: Testimonial transformation (review → story post). (Testimonials into ads)

Day 21: Reshare + reflection.

Days 22–30: Conversion Week

Day 22: "Thinking of selling? Here's what your home could be worth" (lead magnet post).

Day 23: New listing launch blitz.

Day 24: "What $500K buys in [city]" (comparison carousel — high share rate).

Day 25: Behind-the-scenes: negotiation win (anonymized).

Day 26: First-time buyer myth-busting.

Day 27: Community event promo or recap.

Day 28: "Ask me anything" Q&A promo.

Day 29: Month-in-review: numbers, lessons, gratitude.

Day 30: Next month's teaser + follower thank-you.

The Batch-Day System (4 Hours → 30 Days)

Don't create daily. Create monthly, in one session:

Hour 1 — Listings & authority (8 posts). Run your listing prompts, market stat prompts, and explainer prompts. Save drafts.

Hour 2 — Social core (10 posts). Content calendar prompt generates the captions; you add photos. Carousel outlines, Reel hooks, story ideas.

Hour 3 — Email & nurture (6 pieces). Newsletter draft, 3 follow-up emails, 2 past-client touchpoints. (Email sequences)

Hour 4 — Ads & video (6 pieces). Ad variations, video scripts, hooks. (Video guide, Facebook ads)

Schedule everything. The rest of the month, you just engage with comments — 15 minutes a day.

Measuring What Matters

Track weekly: saves (intent), shares (reach), profile visits (interest), DMs (leads). Ignore vanity likes. After 30 days, double down on your top 3 formats and cut the bottom 2. The plan evolves with your data.

Repurposing: One Idea, Five Posts

Never let a good idea die as one post. AI repurposes everything:

The market stat becomes: the Instagram post, the Reel script, the story poll ("are you surprised?"), the email snippet, the X post.

The listing becomes: the tour Reel, the carousel (5 features), the caption post, the story countdown, the email announcement.

The client story becomes: the feed post, the Reel narration, the testimonial graphic, the website update, the ad creative.

The prompt: "Repurpose this [post/video/email] into 4 additional formats: [specify]. Keep the core message, adapt to each format's conventions."

One idea, five touchpoints. Content multiplication is how small accounts look omnipresent.

Content Pillars by Agent Type

Listing agents: 40% listings, 25% market authority, 20% local, 10% personal, 5% social proof.

Buyer agents: 35% education, 25% listings (tours), 20% local, 10% personal, 10% social proof.

New agents: 30% education (learning in public), 25% personal (your journey), 20% local, 15% market, 10% listings (borrowed with permission).

Luxury agents: 40% listings (cinematic), 25% lifestyle, 20% market, 10% personal, 5% social proof.

AI customizes the 30-day plan to your type: "Adapt this content plan for a [buyer agent] in [market]: adjust the pillars and give me week 1's 7 posts."
