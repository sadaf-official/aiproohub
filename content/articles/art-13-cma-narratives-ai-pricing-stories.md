## Why Narratives Beat Numbers

Sellers don't buy comps; they buy confidence. A stack of numbers says "here's data." A narrative says "here's what your home is worth, here's why, and here's how we'll get it." The story carries the number.

## The 4-Part CMA Narrative Structure

**Part 1 — The market story:** "Maplewood has shifted. Inventory is up 15%, but well-priced homes still sell in 12 days. The market isn't cold — it's selective."

**Part 2 — The comp story:** Not just 5 comps — the story of each. "123 Oak sold for $510K in 9 days because of the renovated kitchen. 456 Pine sat for 60 days at $540K because it was overpriced from day one."

**Part 3 — Your home's story:** "Your home has Oak's kitchen and Pine's lot — positioned at $525K, it's the best value in the neighborhood."

**Part 4 — The strategy story:** "We launch Thursday, broker open Friday, public open house weekend. Priced at $525K, we expect 8–12 showings and offers by Tuesday."

AI drafts all four parts from your comp data. You refine with your local knowledge.

## The Prompt Framework

> "Here are 5 comparable sales: [paste comp data]. Write a seller-facing CMA narrative: (1) 100-word market overview, (2) 2-sentence story for each comp explaining WHY it sold at that price, (3) positioning analysis for [subject property], (4) recommended list price with reasoning, (5) 3-sentence launch strategy. Confident, clear, no jargon."

## Handling the "Zillow Says" Objection

Every seller has a Zestimate. AI prepares your response:

> "Draft a kind, data-driven explanation of why Zillow's estimate differs from our CMA: automated vs. human analysis, what algorithms miss (condition, upgrades, lot specifics), and 3 questions to ask about any automated valuation."

Respect the emotion, lead with math. The seller who understands the number defends the number.

## The Pricing Conversation (AI-Prepared)

**Overpriced seller:** the "pricing experiment" proposal — "Let's test $550K for 14 days with these marketing commitments, then reassess with showing data." AI drafts the proposal.

**Underpriced fear:** "Priced at market, we create competition. Competition creates over-asking offers." AI builds the comparable examples.

**The range strategy:** AI analyzes whether a price range or exact price serves this property better, with local precedent.

## Visual CMA Summaries

AI also structures the one-page visual summary: the number, the 3 supporting comps, the strategy — designed for the seller to photograph and show their spouse. The prompts include formatting instructions for maximum clarity.

## The Listing Presentation Flow

The CMA narrative is the centerpiece of a winning listing presentation:

1. **You** (2 min): who you are, why you're different
2. **Marketing plan** (5 min): what you'll do (AI-drafted, customized)
3. **CMA narrative** (10 min): the pricing story
4. **Objection handling** (5 min): commission, timeline, concerns ([scripts](/blog/art-18-objection-handling-ai-scripts.html))
5. **Close** (3 min): the ask

Twenty-five minutes. Structured. Confident. The listing is yours.

## The Price Improvement Conversation

When the market speaks and the price must move, AI helps you deliver the news with data, not drama:

> "Draft a price improvement conversation for [address]: current price, days on market, showing feedback themes, comparable price reductions, and 3 new-price options with projected outcomes. Tone: collaborative strategist, not bearer of bad news. Include the repositioning announcement copy."

The key reframe: it's not a "reduction" (defeat) — it's a "repositioning" (strategy). AI writes the announcement that re-energizes the listing: new price, new photos, new story. Buyers who passed at the old price reconsider at the new one.

## Preparing for the Appraisal Gap

Smart CMAs anticipate appraisal. AI builds the appraisal-prep packet:

- The 3 strongest comps, formatted for the appraiser
- The upgrades list with costs (what the algorithm can't see)
- The neighborhood narrative (why this street commands more)

The prompt: "Build an appraiser packet for [address]: top 3 comps with adjustment reasoning, documented upgrades with approximate costs, and a 100-word neighborhood value narrative."

Listings that anticipate appraisal close smoother. The agents who prepare these packets are the agents whose deals survive.
