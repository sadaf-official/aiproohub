*Meta: How real estate agents grow on Instagram with AI — content pillars, caption formulas, Reel scripts, hashtag strategy, and the 30-day plan.*

Instagram is the modern agent's storefront. But "post more" isn't a strategy. This is: content pillars, AI-generated captions, Reel scripts, and a system that takes 30 minutes a day.

## The 5 Content Pillars

1. **Listings** (30%): tours, features, just-listed, just-sold. Your inventory, showcased.
2. **Education** (25%): buyer/seller tips, market explainers, myth-busting. Authority content.
3. **Local** (20%): neighborhoods, businesses, community. The "local expert" proof.
4. **Personal** (15%): behind-the-scenes, day-in-the-life, values. The human connection.
5. **Social proof** (10%): testimonials, closings, wins. The trust builder.

AI generates ideas for all five from a single weekly prompt: "Generate 7 post ideas for this week: 2 listings, 2 education, 1 local, 1 personal, 1 social proof — customized for [your market]."

## Caption Formulas (AI-Filled)

**The listing caption:** hook (first line = the scroll-stopper) + 3 feature highlights + lifestyle line + CTA ("DM 'TOUR' for a private showing") + hashtags.

**The educational caption:** myth + truth + takeaway + question (questions drive comments).

**The personal caption:** story + lesson + invitation ("what's your experience?").

AI writes all three formats in your voice ([voice training](/blog/art-11-make-ai-sound-like-you-voice-training.html)). You add the photo and post.

## Reel Scripts (The Reach Engine)

Reels get 2–3x the reach of static posts. The AI Reel script formula:

- **0–3 sec:** hook (text on screen + spoken). "This $450K house has a $700K kitchen."
- **3–20 sec:** the tour/highlights. Quick cuts, one feature per cut.
- **20–30 sec:** CTA. "Follow for daily [city] tours."

AI writes 30 scripts in one batch session. Film 5 in an hour (same location, outfit changes optional). Schedule across the month. ([Full video guide](/blog/art-17-video-scripts-agents-reels-tiktok.html))

## Hashtag Strategy (2026)

Mix 3 tiers per post: 3–5 large (#realestate, 20M+ posts), 5–8 medium (#maplewoodrealestate, 10K–500K), 3–5 niche (#maplewoodcolonial, under 10K). AI generates the mix per post — location-specific tags are where discovery happens.

## Stories & Highlights (The Trust Layer)

Daily stories: polls ("which kitchen?"), Q&A stickers, behind-the-scenes, countdowns. AI writes the story text overlays and poll questions in weekly batches.

Highlights: organize into "Listings," "Testimonials," "Neighborhoods," "Tips," "About Me." Your profile becomes a 24/7 open house.

## The 30-Minute Daily Routine

- **10 min:** engage (comments, DMs, other agents' posts)
- **10 min:** post today's content (pre-made in batch sessions)
- **10 min:** stories (2–3, AI-captioned)

Consistency beats virality. Daily presence for 90 days transforms an account.

## Analytics: What to Track (And Ignore)

**Track weekly:**
- **Saves:** intent signal. High saves = content worth repeating.
- **Shares:** reach signal. High shares = content worth boosting.
- **Profile visits:** interest signal. Spikes mean something worked.
- **DMs:** lead signal. The metric that matters most.
- **Follows from content:** which posts convert viewers to followers.

**Ignore:**
- **Likes:** vanity. Correlates with nothing.
- **Follower count alone:** 1,000 engaged locals beat 10,000 random followers.
- **Viral views without follows:** entertainment, not business.

Monthly, feed your stats to AI: "Analyze my Instagram month: [stats]. Top 3 content types, bottom 2, and next month's adjustments." Data-driven content beats gut-feel content.

## The Competitor Audit (Learn From Winners)

Monthly, study 3 thriving agent accounts:

Adapt, never copy. Your market, your voice, their proven tactics. The audit takes 20 minutes and generates a month of ideas.
