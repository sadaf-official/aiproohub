## The 3-Second Hook: Why Most Agent Videos Die Instantly

The scroll is a reflex. Your viewer is not deciding whether your video is good; they are deciding whether to keep moving their thumb. That decision takes about three seconds, and it is made on the opening frame plus the first line. Most agent videos open with a logo, a wave, or "So today I wanted to talk about..." All three are thumb-moving triggers.

Great video hooks for TikTok for realtors follow five proven formulas:

**The bold claim.** "This $400k house has something $800k houses don't." The viewer stays to find out what.

**The number.** "3 things your landlord doesn't want you to know." Numbers promise a finite, scannable payoff.

**The question.** "Why are homes in Riverside selling in 6 days?" Questions open loops the brain wants closed.

**The myth-bust.** "You do NOT need 20% down. Here's the real number." Contradiction stops scrolls cold.

**The visual shock.** Start mid-action: opening a front door, a jaw-dropping kitchen reveal, a price on screen. No talking, just motion.

Generate hooks in batches with AI, then film only the winners:

**Prompt: Write 10 hooks for my video**

> "I'm a real estate agent filming a [30-second TikTok/Reel] about [topic: e.g., new listing at 42 Oak St, $450k, 3-bed]. Write 10 opening hooks, each under 12 words, each using a different formula: bold claim, number, question, myth-bust, or visual shock with on-screen text. No greetings or introductions. Punchy and specific."

## AI Video Scripts for Real Estate TikTok and Reels: The Formula System

Every converting short-form script follows the same three-act anatomy, whether it is 15 seconds or 60:

**Act 1: Hook (0-3 seconds).** One of the five formulas above. On-screen text repeats the hook for sound-off viewers, because most watch muted at first.

**Act 2: Value (3-20 seconds).** Exactly one idea, delivered fast. One listing highlight tour. One myth busted. One tip. Agents fail here by cramming five ideas into one video; the algorithm reads the resulting drop-off as "boring."

**Act 3: CTA (final 5 seconds).** One clear next step. Not three. One.

This formula is the backbone of real estate video marketing on short-form. Longer [video scripts for agents](/blog/art-17-video-scripts-agents-reels-tiktok.html) allow story arcs; TikTok and Reels reward compression: hook, one idea, CTA, done.

**Prompt: Build my short-form video script**

Suggest on-screen text for the hook and beats. Under 75 spoken words. Conversational, energetic, no jargon."

## Proven AI Video Scripts for Real Estate TikTok and Reels: Tours, Myth-Busting, Day-in-the-Life

You do not need endless Instagram Reels ideas for agents. You need five formats on rotation:

**1. The POV listing tour.** Walk through the home, phone in hand, like showing a friend. Hook on the wildest feature ("The backyard on this $450k listing is unreal"), three rooms, CTA to DM for a showing. Raw beats polished.

**2. The myth-bust.** "Myth: you need perfect credit to buy." Thirty seconds of correction. These earn saves, and saves are the algorithm's love language.

**3. Day-in-the-life.** "Showing 6 homes in 4 hours." Fast cuts, honest moments. Builds the know-like-trust that makes someone DM you months later.

**4. The 30-second market update.** One stat, one translation, one implication. "Inventory in Riverside just dropped 20%. Translation: fewer choices, more competition. If you're buying, speed matters right now." Film monthly; it positions you as the local economist.

**5. The comment reply.** Find a viral real estate video, stitch or duet it with your local take. "This national advice doesn't work in our market, and here's why." Borrowed attention, local authority.

## TikTok vs Reels: Platform Differences That Change Your Script

Same script on both platforms underperforms on at least one. Adjust for how each platform actually behaves:

**TikTok rewards raw and fast.** Car-filmed talking-head videos beat studio polish. Trends, sounds, and comment culture drive reach. Jump cuts every 2-3 seconds. Keyword-rich captions for TikTok search. Younger audience: first-time buyer content wins.

**Reels rewards polished and saveable.** Higher production value reads professional on Instagram. Educational videos ("5 closing costs explained") earn saves, and saves drive distribution. Location tags beat hashtags. Slightly older audience: more move-up buyers and sellers.

**Practical script adjustments:**
- Hook: TikTok can be edgier ("Your landlord is praying you don't see this"); Reels stays professional-warm.
- Length: TikTok sweet spot is 21-34 seconds; Reels performs well at 30-45 seconds for educational content.
- CTA: TikTok pushes comments ("Comment MAP and I'll send the list"); Reels pushes DMs and profile visits.
- Posting: TikTok rewards volume (1-2 daily); Reels rewards consistency (4-5 weekly) with stronger evergreen discovery.

Industry research summarized by [Amplifiles](https://www.amplifiles.ai/use-cases/for-real-estate-agents) finds listings with video earn far more inquiries and viewing time than photo-only listings. Mention it at your next listing appointment: sellers notice which agents offer video.

## CTAs That Turn Viewers Into Leads

Views are vanity. Leads are the business. Short-form CTAs must move the viewer off the app, because followers do not pay commission.

**The keyword DM.** "DM me the word TOUR and I'll send the full video walkthrough." One word, low friction, starts a conversation you own. Set up an auto-reply with the link plus one qualifying question.

**The comment bait.** "Comment PRICE and I'll reply with what it listed for." Comments boost distribution while capturing intent publicly.

**The save nudge.** "Save this for when you're house hunting." Saves boost reach and put your content in their saved folder for later.

**The profile funnel.** Your bio link should go to a single landing page: valuation, listings, or a "work with me" form. Not your homepage. One page, one action.

**The follow for series.** "Follow for part 2: the kitchen reveal." Series create returning viewers, and returning viewers become clients.

Every CTA needs a capturing destination. Route DMs and comments into your [five-minute follow-up system](/blog/art-34-five-minute-lead-follow-up-ai-prompts.html) so a 9 p.m. comment becomes a 9:05 p.m. conversation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long should my real estate TikToks and Reels be?**

Aim for 21 to 45 seconds. TikTok favors the shorter end; Reels tolerates slightly longer educational videos. Under 15 seconds rarely delivers enough value. When in doubt, cut a beat rather than adding one.

**Do I need to show my face on camera?**

It helps, because people hire people, but it's not required. POV tours, on-screen text videos, and voiceovers all perform. If camera shyness stops you from posting, start faceless and add yourself once the habit sticks.

**How often should I post?**

TikTok rewards 1 to 2 videos daily; Reels rewards 4 to 5 quality posts weekly. Consistency beats volume over months. One video a week for a year outperforms thirty videos in a week followed by silence.

**Can AI write the whole script, or do I edit?**

AI writes the structure and first draft; you add the local details only you know: the street name, the school, the coffee shop. Never post a script with unverified placeholder facts.
