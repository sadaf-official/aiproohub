## How Retargeting Works (The 60-Second Version)

A visitor browses your listings. A pixel tags them. Later, scrolling Facebook or Instagram, they see YOUR ad for the property they viewed. Familiarity + timing = the highest-converting ad dollar in real estate.

## The 3-Ad Sequence (Steal This)

**Ad 1 — The reminder (days 1–7):** "Still thinking about 123 Maple?" Same property photo, gentle nudge. AI writes 5 variants.

**Ad 2 — The value-add (days 8–21):** new information — "Price just reduced," "New photos added," "Open house Sunday." AI writes the update angles.

**Ad 3 — The closer (days 22–45):** social proof + urgency. "3 showings this week — schedule yours before it's gone." AI writes the urgency without pressure.

After 45 days, rotate to brand ads (testimonials, market authority) — staying visible without stalking.

## Audience Segmentation (AI-Planned)

Not all visitors are equal. AI segments your retargeting:

- **Listing viewers:** property-specific ads (the sequence above)
- **Homepage visitors:** brand introduction ads ("Meet [name], [city]'s [specialty] agent")
- **Blog readers:** authority ads ("Get the monthly market report")
- **Lead magnet downloaders:** consultation booking ads

Each segment gets its own copy. AI writes all four tracks in one session.

## The Copy Framework

> "Write 5 retargeting ad variants for [visitor viewed listing X]: Ad 1 (gentle reminder, days 1-7), Ad 2 (new information angle, days 8-21), Ad 3 (social proof + soft urgency, days 22-45). Each: headline (10 words), body (2 lines), CTA. Warm, never creepy — acknowledge they visited without being invasive."

The "never creepy" instruction matters. "We saw you looking" is stalker; "Still interested in 123 Maple?" is helpful. AI nails the tone with the right guardrails.

## Frequency & Budget

**Frequency cap:** 3–5 impressions per person per week. More = annoyance.

**Budget:** $5–10/day per audience segment. Retargeting is cheap because the audience is small and warm.

**Duration:** 45 days per sequence, then rotate creative. Fresh ads prevent banner blindness.

AI builds your retargeting calendar: which ads run when, budget per segment, rotation schedule.

## The Retargeting Mistakes (Avoid These)

**No frequency cap:** 20 impressions a day = blocked and annoyed. Cap at 3–5/week.

**Stale creative:** same ad for 90 days = invisible. Rotate every 45 days.

**No exclusion:** retargeting current clients with "looking to buy?" ads = embarrassing. Exclude converters.

**Generic copy:** "Check out our listings!" converts nothing. Property-specific, always.

**No landing page match:** ad promises X, page shows Y. AI ensures message match across the click.
