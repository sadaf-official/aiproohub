Every agent asks the same question: *which AI should I actually use?* ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini are all free to start, and all can write a listing description. But they have real differences that matter for real estate marketing.

## The short answer

- **ChatGPT** — best all-rounder, biggest ecosystem of real-estate prompt libraries
- **Claude** — best long-form writing; listing descriptions and newsletters feel the most natural
- **Gemini** — best when your marketing lives in Google's world (Docs, Gmail) and solid at quick drafts

If you only pick one, pick the one you'll actually open every day. Consistency beats features.

## Listing descriptions: Claude takes the crown

For the core job — turning property facts into an MLS-ready description — Claude consistently produces the most natural-sounding copy with the least editing. Its longer context window also means you can paste in an entire CMA or a long feature sheet without it losing track.

ChatGPT is a close second and often punchier, which suits ad headlines. Gemini is fine for first drafts but tends to need more revision passes.

## Ad copy and social: ChatGPT's playground

For Facebook ads, Instagram captions, and Reel scripts, ChatGPT's punchier default voice works well, and its huge user base means more realtor-tested prompt templates exist for it than for anything else. When in doubt, the crowd has already solved your problem for ChatGPT.

## Email sequences: a tie

All three handle buyer nurture and seller update emails competently. The differentiator isn't the tool — it's the prompt. A structured prompt with your voice notes ("warm but professional, never pushy, sign off with my tagline") matters 10x more than which logo is on the tab.

## The fair-housing rule applies everywhere

Whichever tool you choose, the compliance burden is yours. All three models can occasionally produce language that steers toward or away from protected classes if your prompt invites it. Always screen AI output the same way you'd screen your own writing: describe the *home*, never the *ideal resident*.

## How to decide

1. **Start with one.** Don't split your attention across three tools.
2. **Match it to your workflow.** Heavy Gmail user? Gemini. Write long newsletters? Claude. Want the biggest prompt library? ChatGPT.
3. **Build a prompt library.** The tool matters less than having 20–30 proven prompts you reuse. That's where the real time savings compound.

The best AI for realtors isn't the smartest model — it's the one paired with great prompts, used every single day.
